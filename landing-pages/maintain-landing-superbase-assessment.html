The Problem

The Knowledge That Built
Your System Is Walking Out

A 2025 Horváth study of 200 companies found that only 8% of S/4HANA transformations finish on schedule. Most post-mortems point to scope or resourcing. Very few ask what walked out the door before anyone thought to document it.

Only 34% of organisations have fully completed their S/4HANA move. The majority are still mid-transition — dependent on ECC-era experts approaching the end of their careers.

SAP entry-level openings declined 35% in a single year. The pipeline is thinning at precisely the moment the senior exit wave is accelerating.

When experienced consultants leave, AI doesn't inherit the context. It inherits the process as documented. Which is often not the same thing.

Most organisations don't have a capture plan. They have a resourcing plan. There's a difference — and the gap shows up at cutover.

The Deliverable

What the Assessment
Produces

  • 1
    Structured Design BaselineA documented record of your SAP configuration decisions, process exceptions, and the logic behind how the system actually runs.
  • 2
    Knowledge Gap MapAn identification of which areas have adequate documentation and which rely entirely on people who could leave.
  • 3
    Capture Priority ListA prioritised list of the knowledge most at risk, ranked by programme impact and likelihood of loss.
  • 4
    Transfer RecommendationA practical recommendation for how to close the highest-risk gaps — structured for delivery teams, not just leadership.
The Process

How a Superbase Assessment Works

1
IngestWe take your existing system documentation — design specs, configuration guides, process maps, anything that exists — and feed it into Superbase.
2
AnalyseSuperbase processes the documentation and identifies gaps, dependencies, undocumented decisions, and high-risk knowledge areas.
3
StructureWe produce a structured design baseline — organised, searchable, and built to be maintained as the system evolves.
4
ValidateOur team reviews the baseline with your key people while they're still available — filling gaps and confirming accuracy before sign-off.
