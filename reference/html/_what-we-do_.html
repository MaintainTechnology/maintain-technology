What We Do

At Maintain Technology, we bring order to chaos and momentum to complexity. We’re not consultants — we’re your enterprise systems strikeforce, deployed to lead, stabilise and deliver on your most high-stakes transformation missions.

With a multi-disciplinary crew that operates with tactical precision, deep technical firepower and boots-on-the-ground leadership, we drive outcomes that matter without compromising your timeline, budget or your team’s wellbeing.

Our Core Capabilities

Strategic Leadership & Program Command

We take the helm of complex programs from day one — ensuring strategic alignment, stakeholder buy-in, and measurable impact. Our program leaders stabilise delivery, restore confidence, and keep your mission moving forward.

Architecture & Solution Design

Whether you're deploying SAP S/4HANA, SuccessFactors, or both, we architect systems that are scalable, secure, and future-ready. We cut through complexity to align technical build with business value.

Cutover, Deployment & Data Migration

We own the deployment runway — planning and executing seamless go-lives with military-grade precision. We ensure data integrity, business continuity, and operational readiness at every checkpoint.

Master Data Governance & Integration

Clean data. Clear insights. We establish robust governance frameworks and streamline integration across systems, departments, and partners — enabling smarter, faster decisions.

Commercial Operating Model Design

We align enterprise tech with commercial reality — reshaping operating models to boost agility, margins, and service efficiency.

Human-Centred Design

People are at the heart of every transformation. We embed human-centred design to drive system adoption, streamline workflows, and reduce friction in high-change environments.

The Maintain Methodology:

Our 3-Stage Intervention Framework 

Our 3-Stage Intervention Framework 

Our 3-stage intervention model is built for programs under pressure — whether you’re mapping a new mission, recalibrating mid-flight, or pulling a project back from the brink.

Early Stage

Hazard Mapping

When to Deploy: Early-stage planning, RFPs, or deal negotiation

We bring early clarity — aligning stakeholders, de-risking strategy, and setting the mission up for success.
  • Commercial alignment & contract mediation
  • Transformation roadmapping
  • Tech feasibility & proof of concept reviews

Mid-Stage

Course Correction

When to Deploy: Mid-mission misalignment, delays, or governance breakdown 

We stabilise programs, re-engage stakeholders, and restore operational cadence.
  • Delivery audits & recalibration
  • Risk-focused realignment 
  • Vendor coordination & governance reset 

Late Stage

Project Rescue

When to Deploy: Failing programs, missed milestones, stalled teams  

We act fast — deploying rapid-response leadership and a stabilisation strategy that brings projects back online. 
  • Triage & continuity planning
  • Critical path rescue
  • Embedded leadership to lead recovery
