## Users

**Who reads/uses brand output:** enterprise and public-sector decision-makers evaluating a technology delivery partner, plus Maintain's own team producing on-brand material (proposals, decks, social, letterheads, web).

**Buyer audience (the people the brand must convince):**
- Enterprise firms with 500+ employees across Australia
- Government & public-sector agencies — defence, infrastructure, transport, health
- Mining and natural-resources companies
- Organisations mid-transformation on SAP S/4HANA or SuccessFactors

**Key decision-makers:** CIOs, CTOs, Heads of Digital Transformation; Programme Managers and SAP Leads; Government procurement officers; Talent Acquisition / HRIS managers.

**Their context:** high-stakes, time-pressured, mission-critical programmes where delay or a wrong hire is expensive. They need to feel *speed + trust + sector credibility* at a glance.

## Product Purpose

Maintain Technology is **Australia's enterprise-systems "strikeforce"** — it deploys mission-ready SAP/enterprise talent and solutions *within 5 days*, specialising in the SAP ecosystem (S/4HANA, SuccessFactors) for critical industries.

- **Mission:** deploy high-impact enterprise solutions and mission-critical talent faster than anyone else.
- **Vision:** be Australia's unstoppable enterprise-systems strikeforce — humanising technology through speed and expertise.
- **Proof points:** 5-day deployment promise; deep defence/government/mining track record; founder-led (Jon Pepper).
- **Legal entity:** The Pep Collective Pty Limited t/a Maintain Tech (ABN 70 646 284 586).

Success for the brand system: every artefact — a slide, a LinkedIn banner, an infographic, a web section — instantly reads as Maintain (dark ground, Risk Orange energy, mountain motif, uppercase authority) and passes as *fast, elite, and trustworthy*.

## Brand Personality

**Three words: Bold. Precise. Relentless.**

Voice pillars (from the guidelines):
1. **Bold & Visionary** — command and clarity; strong claims backed by expertise.
2. **Authoritative but Approachable** — expert and trustworthy, never jargon-heavy.
3. **Purpose-Driven & Action-Oriented** — outcomes, speed, mission-critical challenges.
4. **Energetic & Direct** — get to the point with urgency that inspires confidence.

**Lexicon is military / tactical** and mountain-metaphor: *deploy, strikeforce, mission-ready, tactical, precision, rapid response, mobilise, force multiplier, battle-tested, move mountains.* Taglines in use: **"Made to Move Mountains"**, "#MadetoMoveMountains", "Humanising Tech. Empowering Talent. Delivering Impact." Core belief: *"If there's a way, we find it. If there isn't, we build it."*

Brand values: **We Move Mountains · Do Whatever It Takes · Earned Trust · Bold Thinking, Precise Delivery.**

## Anti-references

- **Not the SaaS-blue / fintech-purple crowd.** Risk Orange was chosen specifically to stand out in a market saturated with blue and purple. Never let a design drift into generic tech-blue.
- **Not soft, pastel, playful, or "friendly-startup."** This is an enterprise strikeforce — no rounded-blob mascots, no candy gradients, no cute.
- **Not corporate-beige / cream-warm-neutral** editorial blandness. The warmth here is Risk Orange energy on a dark ground, not sand/paper backgrounds.
- **Not synthetic people.** No AI-generated portraits in final assets — human photography must feel real and grounded.
- **Not decoration for its own sake.** Every element (orange, caps, mountain) carries meaning: risk, authority, elevation.

## Design Principles

1. **Dark ground, orange energy.** The brand's confident mode is a dark Forge-Blue/Black field with Risk Orange as the single, deliberate charge of energy — used for the mark, CTAs, highlights, and glow. Orange is a scalpel, not a wash.
2. **Speak in capitals; earn every word.** Albert Sans, ALL CAPS, is the voice of authority. Pair short, punchy, tactical copy with generous space — say less, mean more.
3. **The mountain is the throughline.** The wireframe peak motif (and the peak-form logomark) appears across every surface as the connective identifier — always orange-in-front receding to teal/behind.
4. **Precision over decoration.** Sharp, engineered, exact — mirrored in the hard-edged core icon set and tight typography. Nothing is approximate.
5. **Real people, branded by graphic.** Photography stays authentic and natural; the brand is applied *on top* via the wireframe motif, gradient overlays, and orange accents — never a fake in-camera grade.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Target: WCAG 2.1 AA.** Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large/bold text ≥ 3:1. Verified pairings and the one hard failure (Risk Orange as text on light = 2.8:1 → use orange shade `#662600` instead) are documented in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md#3-color) and the token comments.
- **Never rely on orange alone** to carry meaning (colour-blind safety) — pair with label, icon, or weight.
- **Motion is energetic but optional:** every animation must have a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback. The token layer zeroes durations under that query.
- **Inclusive imagery:** diverse, authentic people across ages and roles; grounded, not tokenistic.
